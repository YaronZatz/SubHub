# Neighborhood boundaries

`tel-aviv-neighborhoods.json` is derived from the **Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality** open GIS layer
"שכונות" (layer 511, `gisn.tel-aviv.gov.il/arcgis/rest/services/IView2/MapServer/511`),
downloaded 2026-10-17 and simplified (~8 m tolerance, 5-decimal coordinates).

Source: Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality open data, https://opendatasource.tel-aviv.gov.il —
used under its open data terms (free to share and adapt with attribution). Provided "as is".
